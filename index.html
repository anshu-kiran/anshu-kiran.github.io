Abstract

Anshu Kiran Sharma is a Data Scientist at Pacific Northwest National Laboratory. Her research interests center on natural language processing (NLP), large language models (LLMs), agentic AI, and multi-agent systems, with broader interests in computational linguistics, narrative understanding, and cognitive science.
